How to Use This Calendar

Step 1 — Download your videos

Log in to higgsfield.ai → Generations. All 30 videos are rendering there. Download each one (they're labeled by order). Rename them day-01.mp4 through day-30.mp4.

Step 2 — Schedule in Buffer (free)

  • Go to buffer.com and create a free account
  • Connect your Instagram Business account + TikTok
  • Add each video with its caption from this document
  • Set posts to go out once per day at your peak time (typically 7–9 PM EST)
  • Buffer auto-posts to Instagram · sends a TikTok push notification to your phone to tap "Post" (TikTok API requirement — takes 5 seconds)

Step 3 — Edit captions before posting

  • Replace "Link in bio" with your actual link (e.g. seaandcityrentals.com/guestgrowth)
  • Add your personal story details where you see italics cues
  • Keep the hook line exactly as written — it's crafted to stop the scroll
Pro tip: Pin your best-performing video (whichever gets the most saves/shares by Day 7) at the top of your TikTok profile. Add the GuestGrowth landing page link to your Instagram bio and TikTok bio on Day 1 before you post anything.

Caption structure explained

  • Hook — the first line. Never change this. It determines whether someone stops scrolling.
  • Body — 2–3 sentences of context. You can personalize these with your own numbers.
  • CTA — Call to action. Always ends with "Link in bio." or "DM me [keyword]."
  • Hashtags — paste these below the CTA. Use all of them.

Video IDs (Higgsfield)

Each day card lists the Higgsfield job ID so you can match the right video to the right caption. Find videos by ID in your generations library.
