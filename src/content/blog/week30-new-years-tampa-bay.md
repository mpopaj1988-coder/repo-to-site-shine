New Year's Eve on the Gulf Coast is what you make of it. There are big organized events, beach gatherings, dinner-and-fireworks combos, and the very valid option of a quiet rental with the people you actually like. Here's the lay of the land.

## The Big Public Events

### First Night St. Pete

Downtown St. Pete's family-friendly arts and music festival on December 31. Multiple stages, food vendors, indoor and outdoor performances, and a midnight fireworks show over the waterfront. Genuinely one of the better organized public NYE events in Florida.

### New Year's Eve at Pier 60 (Clearwater Beach)

The Pier 60 nightly festival runs as usual, with a fireworks show over the Gulf at midnight. Crowded but with a built-in beach party energy.

### Tampa Riverwalk

Downtown Tampa puts on fireworks over the Hillsborough River, with the Riverwalk packed with restaurants and bars open late. Curtis Hixon Park is the prime viewing spot.

## Restaurant Reservations

If you want a dinner-and-fireworks plan, book in October. By December the desirable spots — anything with a water view in St. Pete, Tampa, or Clearwater — are completely booked. Specifically:

* The Edison and Datz in Tampa
* Locale Market and Birch & Vine in St. Pete
* Salt Rock Grill and Marina Cantina on the beaches

## The Rental Approach

## Booking Window

New Year's week books out faster than any week except Christmas. By late October, options narrow significantly. By December, you're picking through what's left. Plan accordingly.
