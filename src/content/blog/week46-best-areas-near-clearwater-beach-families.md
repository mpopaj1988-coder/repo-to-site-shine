Clearwater Beach is one of Florida's most popular family vacation destinations, but many visitors are surprised to discover that staying directly on Clearwater Beach isn't always the best option.

Families often find that nearby communities offer more space, less traffic, easier parking, and better vacation rentals while remaining just minutes from the beach.

Here's where families should consider staying.

## Indian Rocks Beach

Indian Rocks Beach is one of the most family-friendly beach towns on Florida's Gulf Coast.

Families love:

* Less crowded beaches
* Easier parking
* Local restaurants
* Relaxed atmosphere
* Beautiful sunsets

Unlike Clearwater Beach, Indian Rocks Beach feels more residential and less commercial. Many families stay here and visit Clearwater Beach when they want additional attractions. ## Largo

Largo is ideal for travelers who want larger homes, private pools, better value, and easy beach access. Many vacation rentals in Largo provide significantly more space than beachfront hotels for the same budget.

## St. Petersburg

Families who enjoy museums, waterfront parks, and dining often choose St. Petersburg.

Popular attractions include:

* St. Pete Pier
* Downtown St. Pete
* Sunken Gardens
* Museums
* Waterfront parks
