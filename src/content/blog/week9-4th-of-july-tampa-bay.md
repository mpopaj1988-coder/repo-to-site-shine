Fourth of July on the Gulf Coast is the real deal. Fireworks over the water, patriotic beach crowds, watercraft parades on the Intracoastal, and the kind of warm summer night that makes you glad to be wherever you are.

## Where the Fireworks Are

### Clearwater Beach

Clearwater Beach hosts one of the biggest Fourth of July celebrations on the Gulf Coast. Fireworks launch from the beach after dark — usually around 9pm — visible from the shore, from boats on the water, and from private balconies along Gulf Blvd. Pier 60 is the epicenter, with live music and events leading up to the show.

### St. Pete Pier

Downtown St. Petersburg puts on a fireworks show over Tampa Bay, typically launched from the waterfront near the St. Pete Pier. The views from the pier, from Vinoy Park, and from waterfront restaurants are excellent. This show attracts a slightly older, more local crowd.

### Safety Harbor / Philippe Park

For a lower-key Fourth, Safety Harbor shoots fireworks over Old Tampa Bay — Philippe Park is a great viewing spot with room to spread out, and the community vibe is warm and festive without the crowds of the big beach events.

## On the Water

If you have access to a boat or can rent one, watching the Fourth of July fireworks from the water is hard to top. Several marinas around Clearwater and St. Pete offer holiday boat rentals — book early, these go fast in June. The Intracoastal also hosts informal boat parades — decorated pontoons, flags, patriotic chaos.

## Practical Tips for the Holiday

- Parking is a nightmare — stay in a vacation rental close to the beach and walk
- Make dinner reservations at least a week ahead
- Bring a blanket and stake out your fireworks spot at least an hour before showtime
- Expect traffic on Gulf Blvd after the fireworks — plan to walk back and wait it out
