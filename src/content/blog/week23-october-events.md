October is when the Gulf Coast event calendar wakes back up after summer. Cooler weather brings festivals outdoors, Halloween turns Ybor City into one of the better street parties in the South, and the food and drink calendar fills in fast.

## Clearwater Jazz Holiday

A long-running multi-day jazz festival held at Coachman Park in downtown Clearwater. National headliners, a beautiful waterfront setting, and a genuinely mixed crowd. Tickets sell out — book accommodation early. [Find Clearwater-area properties at Sea & City Rentals](https://seaandcityrentals.com).

## Ribfest St. Pete

Three days of competition barbecue, live music, and an unreasonable amount of smoked meat at Vinoy Park on the St. Pete waterfront. National BBQ teams compete, the lineup leans country and southern rock, and the views of the bay don't hurt.

## Guavaween (Ybor City)

Tampa's signature Halloween street party in Ybor City. Costumes are not optional, the historic district shuts down to traffic, and the energy is somewhere between Mardi Gras and a giant block party. If you've never done Halloween in Tampa, this is the move.

## Oktoberfest at Local Breweries

Most Tampa Bay breweries do a real Oktoberfest weekend in mid-to-late October — German lagers on tap, food trucks, sometimes live oompah, often outdoor seating that's finally pleasant again. Dunedin and St. Pete have the densest concentration.

## John's Pass Seafood Festival

Last weekend of October at John's Pass Village in Madeira Beach. Fresh Gulf seafood, live music on the water, and a setting that genuinely captures what makes the Gulf Coast different from the rest of Florida.

## Planning Around Events

Festival weekends mean tighter accommodation availability and higher rates closer to the date. Booking 4–6 weeks out gets you the best selection.
